# Wildfire Topic Definition

> **Version: v4.1** - consistency and safety revision of v4.

Topic: `wildfire`.

Eligible exposure: wildfire, bushfire, forest/landscape fire, wildfire or fire-attributed smoke,
fire-attributed PM2.5, smoke-day product, burn area, fire perimeter/proximity, fire evacuation,
or an official disaster declaration attributed to wildfire.

Generic PM2.5, air pollution, haze or smoke without fire attribution is not an eligible wildfire
exposure. Tobacco/cooking smoke, structural/house/industrial fires, burn injuries, fire ecology,
forest management and firefighter physiology alone are not eligible exposure. If fire attribution
is plausible but genuinely unclear, REVIEW.
