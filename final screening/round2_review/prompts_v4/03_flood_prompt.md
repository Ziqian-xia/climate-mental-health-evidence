# Flood Topic Definition

> **Version: v4.1** - consistency and safety revision of v4.

Topic: `flood`.

Eligible exposure: flood, flash/riverine/coastal flood, inundation, river overflow, storm-surge
flooding, flood extent/depth, flooded property/area, flood victims/shelters/displacement, or a
disaster declaration with documented flood attribution.

Heavy precipitation, heavy/extreme rainfall, rainstorm, downpour, pluvial rainfall, monsoon rain,
rainfall anomaly and rainfall-runoff modelling are not eligible flood exposures by themselves.
A cyclone record belongs here only when flooding, inundation or storm surge is stated. Hydrology,
drainage/engineering, property damage, physical injury and infectious disease alone are not eligible.
