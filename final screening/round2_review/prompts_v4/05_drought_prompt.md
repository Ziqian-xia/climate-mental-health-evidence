# Drought Topic Definition

> **Version: v4.1** - consistency and safety revision of v4.

Topic: `drought`.

Eligible exposure: drought, environmentally caused water scarcity/shortage, prolonged dry spell,
drought disaster/declaration, SPEI, PDSI, SPI, rainfall or precipitation deficit framed as drought,
or soil-moisture anomaly framed as drought.

Plant/crop drought stress, irrigation or soil modelling, water quality/sanitation, and water policy
alone are not eligible human drought exposure. Food security, livelihood and economic loss alone
are not eligible mental-health outcomes; a measured eligible psychological outcome must be present.
