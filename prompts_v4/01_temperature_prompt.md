# Temperature Topic Definition

> **Version: v4.1** - consistency and safety revision of v4.

Topic: `temperature`.

Eligible exposure: naturally occurring ambient/environmental heat or cold, heatwave, cold spell,
daily mean/minimum/maximum temperature, apparent temperature, heat index, WBGT, humidex, diurnal
temperature range, or ambient temperature variability.

EXCLUDE as `wrong_exposure` when the thermal condition is staged or machine-generated: climate or
environmental chamber, controlled thermal room, laboratory heat/cold session, cold-pressor,
water immersion, thermal-pain stimulus, refrigerated/freezer workplace, sauna, cryotherapy,
therapeutic heating/cooling, or induced hypothermia. This remains true if the chamber condition is
called "ambient temperature".

Do not exclude outdoor workers or people experiencing naturally occurring outdoor-origin heat/cold
indoors. If the setting is unclear, REVIEW. Body temperature, fever, cellular heat stress, heat-shock
proteins, thermal comfort/building energy, and temperature preference alone are not eligible exposure.
