# Tropical Cyclone Topic Definition

> **Version: v4.1** - consistency and safety revision of v4.

Topic: `cyclone`.

Eligible exposure: tropical cyclone, hurricane, typhoon, tropical storm, a named storm explicitly
identified as one of these, landfall, storm track, central pressure, cyclone wind speed/intensity,
Saffir-Simpson category, or IBTrACS exposure.

Generic storm, thunderstorm, winter storm, tornado, meteorological forecasting, cytokine storm and
brainstorm are not eligible cyclone exposures. When outcomes are pooled across several disasters
including an eligible cyclone and hazard-specific results are unclear, REVIEW.
